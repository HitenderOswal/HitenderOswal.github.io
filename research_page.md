Synthetic CT Generation for Stroke Lesion Segmentation
This work is a part of my ongoing research in the KurtLab.

In order to develop robust deep learning algorithms for automated stroke lesion segmentation in hemorrhagic and ischemic strokes using CT imaging, a sizable, diverse annotated dataset is required. A key aim of the is project to develop latent diffusion models which are able to generate accurate CT images from stroke lesion masks. These models will allow us to synthesize large-scale annotated CT datasets which can supplement existing datasets and improve performance of downstream diagnostic tasks by medical professionals.


Stroke Lesion Segmentation
This work was also a part of my research in the KurtLab.

Accurate identification of stroke lesion boundaries is critical for diagnosis and treatment. For the ISLES Challenge at MICCAI 2024, we applied clinically-informed preprocessing techniques to a stroke segmentation model, helping our team win first place. I have also explored how these preprocessing techniques can improve segmentation in low-resource settings and investigated explainable AI techniques from a Shapley perspective for multi-contrast medical image segmentation.
